/*
 * ============================================================
 *  MOMENTS, SKEWNESS AND KURTOSIS IN C
 * ============================================================
 *
 * GOAL: describe the SHAPE of the distribution of one column
 * (by default, Attendance) from StudentPerformanceFactors.csv.
 *
 * CSV COLUMNS: Gender, Attendance, Hours_Studied, Exam_Score
 * We only need the 2nd column, Attendance.
 *
 * THE MATH:
 *
 *   Raw moment about the origin (k-th):
 *       mu_k' = (1/n) * sum(x_i ^ k)
 *
 *   Central moment about the mean (k-th):
 *       mu_k  = (1/n) * sum((x_i - mean) ^ k)
 *
 *   Coefficient of skewness:
 *       gamma1 = mu3 / (mu2 ^ 1.5)
 *       beta1  = gamma1 ^ 2
 *
 *   Coefficient of kurtosis:
 *       beta2  = mu4 / (mu2 ^ 2)
 *       gamma2 = beta2 - 3        (this is "excess kurtosis")
 *
 * INTERPRETATION THRESHOLDS:
 *   Skewness (gamma1):
 *       |gamma1| < 0.5        -> approximately symmetric
 *       0.5 <= |gamma1| < 1   -> moderately skewed
 *       |gamma1| >= 1         -> highly skewed
 *       (positive = right-skewed, negative = left-skewed)
 *
 *   Kurtosis (gamma2, excess):
 *       gamma2 ~ 0   -> mesokurtic  (normal-like peak)
 *       gamma2 > 0   -> leptokurtic (sharper peak, heavier tails)
 *       gamma2 < 0   -> platykurtic (flatter peak, lighter tails)
 */
